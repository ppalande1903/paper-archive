How it
works

Upload a PDF and an AI model reads the whole paper, then retells it the same way every time, so you learn the real terminology without needing the background.

Add a paper

All you need is a PDF. No account and no API key: the explainer is written by a small AI model that runs right in your browser, or by Claude when you run Paper Archive on your own computer. It takes a few minutes.

  • Free, in your browser. The model (Qwen3.5) downloads once, about 1 GB, then runs on your device’s graphics chip. It needs a recent Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop.
  • Claude, with approval. Claude writes much fuller explainers. On the live site it runs on the owner’s API credit, so you ask for access in the add-paper dialog and get a personal link. Or download Paper Archive and start it with python3 server.py to use your own Claude plan.
  • You choose what’s kept. When an explainer is ready, you’re asked whether to keep it on this device or only for this session.
  • Your PDF isn’t stored. In the browser it never leaves your device; Claude reads it from a temporary folder that’s deleted straight after. Only the finished explainer is kept, and only if you say so.
